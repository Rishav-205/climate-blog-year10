🌱 What is Climate Change?

Climate change refers to long-term changes in the Earth's temperature and weather patterns. Human activities such as burning fossil fuels, cutting down forests and pollution are major causes of modern climate change.

🌎 Our Changing Planet

🌳 Forests

Forests absorb carbon dioxide and help regulate the Earth's climate. Protecting forests is important for our future.

🌊 Oceans

Oceans help control Earth's temperature and support millions of people and animals.

🏔️ Nature

Changes in temperature and rainfall can affect plants, animals and natural ecosystems.

🌿 Green Planet

Planting and protecting trees can help remove carbon dioxide from the atmosphere.

💡 Did You Know?

Small actions such as saving electricity, reducing waste, planting trees and protecting nature can help create a healthier environment.

🇫🇯 Climate Change and Fiji

Fiji is a Pacific island nation that is vulnerable to the effects of climate change. Rising sea levels, coastal flooding, stronger storms and changes in rainfall can affect communities, agriculture and the environment.

🌱 Protect Our Planet

Climate change is a global challenge, and everyone can help protect our environment.
