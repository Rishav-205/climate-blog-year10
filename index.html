🌱 What is Climate Change?

Climate change refers to long-term changes in the Earth's temperature and weather patterns. Human activities such as burning fossil fuels and cutting down forests are major causes of modern climate change.

🔥 Causes of Climate Change

Some major causes include burning coal, oil and gas, deforestation, pollution and some industrial activities. These activities increase greenhouse gases in the atmosphere.

🌡️ Effects of Climate Change

Climate change can contribute to rising temperatures, changing rainfall patterns, stronger heatwaves, melting ice and rising sea levels. It can also affect people, animals and ecosystems.

🇫🇯 Climate Change and Fiji

Fiji is a Pacific island nation that is vulnerable to climate-related impacts such as coastal flooding, erosion and changes in weather patterns. Protecting our environment is important for the future of Fiji.

🌳 What Can We Do?

We can help by planting trees, reducing waste, saving electricity, using resources responsibly and keeping our surroundings clean.

🌍 Conclusion

Climate change is a serious environmental challenge. Everyone has a role to play in protecting our planet. By making responsible choices today, we can help create a safer and healthier future for the next generation.
